Frequently Asked Questions

What is CraftHub?

+

CraftHub is a platform dedicated to celebrating and preserving traditional handicraftsmanship by connecting skilled artisans with customers who appreciate authentic, handmade products.

How long does shipping take?

+

We typically ship orders within 2-3 business days. Delivery usually takes 5-7 business days depending on your location. Free shipping is available on orders above ₹500.

What is your return policy?

+

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products. If you're not satisfied, simply contact us for a hassle-free return or refund.

Are your products eco-friendly?

+

Yes! We are committed to sustainability. All our products are made using eco-friendly materials and ethical practices that support both artisans and the environment.

Can I customize products?

+

Yes! Many of our products can be customized. Please contact us with your specific requirements, and we'll work with our artisans to create something unique for you.

How can I support artisans?

+

By purchasing from CraftHub, you directly support local artisans. A portion of every sale goes to support fair wages and sustainable practices for craftspeople.
