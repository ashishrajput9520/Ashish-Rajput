ABOUT SUPER GOLDSTAR MULTI LEVEL MARKETING PRIVATE LIMITED

SUPER GOLDSTAR MULTI LEVEL MARKETING PRIVATE LIMITED is a destination for finely crafted Handi Craft glassware, where elegance, creativity, and craftsmanship come together. Our collection features beautifully designed wine glasses, decorative glass items, and handcrafted drinkware that elevate everyday moments and special occasions. Each Handi Craft product is thoughtfully created with attention to detail, balance, and finish. From modern minimal designs to artistic handcrafted styles, our glassware reflects quality, durability, and refined aesthetics. At SUPER GOLDSTAR MULTI LEVEL MARKETING PRIVATE LIMITED, we believe glassware is more than just utility — it is an experience. Our mission is to offer unique, stylish, and premium Jandi Craft products that add charm to dining, gifting, and home décor.

Our mission is to preserve traditional crafts and support local artisans while bringing authentic, high-quality handicrafts to your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SUPER GOLDSTAR MULTI LEVEL MARKETING PRIVATE LIMITED?

+

SUPER GOLDSTAR MULTI LEVEL MARKETING PRIVATE LIMITED is a platform dedicated to celebrating and preserving traditional handicraftsmanship by connecting skilled artisans with customers who appreciate authentic, handmade products.

How long does shipping take?

+

We typically ship orders within 2-3 business days. Delivery usually takes 5-7 business days depending on your location. Free shipping is available on orders above ₹500.

What is your return policy?

+

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products. If you're not satisfied, simply contact us for a hassle-free return or refund.

Are your products eco-friendly?

+

Yes! We are committed to sustainability. All our products are made using eco-friendly materials and ethical practices that support both artisans and the environment.

Can I customize products?

+

Yes! Many of our products can be customized. Please contact us with your specific requirements, and we'll work with our artisans to create something unique for you.

How can I support artisans?

+

By purchasing from SUPER GOLDSTAR MULTI LEVEL MARKETING PRIVATE LIMITED, you directly support local artisans. A portion of every sale goes to support fair wages and sustainable practices for craftspeople.
